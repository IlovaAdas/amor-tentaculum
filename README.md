# Amor Tentaculum
A RimWorld Tentacle Mod

This mod was originally created by MrCastle69 (who is MIA) and was maintained by IcePokeDude.  I (PahandaBear) am taking it over with permission from IcePokeDude, as it needs some love - just like these tentacles do.

At the moment, this mod is EXTREMELY a Work In Progress.  The original mod was unfinished and had quite a few bugs.  I'm going to work on fixing those bugs and then start to add additional features.  Please make use of the Github Issues feature to report any problems you have or suggest things you'd like to see.  I can't promise that I'll be able to implement all of them (I'm still fairly new to RimWorld modding), but I'll do my best.

The tentacle assets are created by [ScorchedRose9](https://linktr.ee/Scorched_Rose9) (commission her!), who has been a huge help in creating this mod.

Many thanks also to the RJW Discord community, who have been excellent for ideas, testing, bug fixes, and general support. You folks are awesome.

# Tentacle Bestiary

## Neutral Tentacles

### Amor Squirmer
The most basic type of tentacle. Mindless, writing, and not able to survive outside of a host body.  Primarily used as a resource for pawn and animal abilities.

### Amor Larva
A newly-born tentacle that can be triggered to grow into one of several different types of mature tentacles once it has reached sufficient age.

## Male Tentacles

### Amor Grazer
Research Tier: Basic

A non-combat mass of tentacles that can be butchered for tentacle meat (food) and the soft flesh leather substitute.  Essentially livestock.

### Amor Striker
Research Tier: Intermediate

A combat-focused mass of tentacles, designed to latch on to and incapacitate hostile enemies with attacks to their genitalia.

### Amor Abductor
Research Tier: Advanced

A non-combat mass of tentacles, designed to target downed enemies and transport them to the hive as prisoners.  Can perform general hauling tasks as well.

## Female Tentacles

### Amor Brood Mother
Research Tier: Basic

A non-combat mass of tentacles that focuses entirely on incubating and laying Amor Larva eggs, which will eventually grow into other types of tentacles.  Has combat abilities, but due to its essential nature to the hive, should not engage in combat unless necessary.

### Amor Spawner
Research Tier: Basic

A non-combat mass of tentacles, designed to help grow Amor Squirmers to empower Amor Hosts' abilities.  Grows and lays Amor Squirmer eggs.

### Amor Producer
Research Tier: Intermediate

A non-combat mass of tentacles that can be harvested for hard and soft resin resources (per normal animal mechanics) used to build other things.

### Amor Brewer
Research Tier: Intermediate

A non-combat mass of tentacles that produces drugs and chemicals regularly.  Can be switched between various production targets.

### Amor Hunter
Research Tier: Advanced

A combat-focused mass of tentacles, designed to protect the hive from external threats with debilitating ranged attacks.

## Boss Tentacles
These are the most powerful tentacle types, but in order to unlock them, you will need to craft a special building to summon them (ala mechanitor bosses).  They will drop an item that can be used to unlock the research to make some of your own, as well as being required for the construction.  Note that these will be "humanoid" Tentacle types - and as such, will require a host body that will be consumed as part of the transformation process, so you'll probably want to use these on prisoners.

### Amor Defender
A combat-focused, male humanoid tentacle, designed to protect the hive from external threats with powerful melee attacks and the ability to absorb high amounts of damage.

When constructed for your own colony, can be trained to perform hauling tasks.

### Amor Valkyrie
A combat-focused, female humanoid tentacle, intended as a vanguard striker for the hive.  Has powerful attacks, some psychic abilities, and can spawn minion tentacles to swarm enemies.

When constructed for your own colony, will emit one of several psychic auras that will buff nearby friendly pawns or debuff nearby hostiles.

# Host Types

## Male Hosts

### Amor Servant
Research Tier: Basic
A male host specialized for labor and crafting.  Minimal combat potential, but can be used to store Amor Squirmers for use by other host pawns.

### Amor Baron
Research Tier: Intermediate

A male host specialized for defense, providing buffs to other host and tentacle pawns.  Can act as supplementary storage for Amor Squirmers.  Uses stored Amor Squirmers to power its abilities.

### Amor Knight
Research Tier: Advanced

A male host specialized for melee combat, at the cost of intelligence and work proficiency.  Uses stored Amor Squirmers to power its abilities.

### Amor Rogue
Research Tier: Advanced

A male host specialized for stealth and reconnaissance.  Minimal combat potential, but naturally difficult to see and can become mostly invisible for a short time.  Uses stored Amor Squirmers to power its abilities.

## Female Hosts

### Amor Queen
Research Tier: Core

The strongest of the female host types.  Grows and lays Amor Brood Mother eggs to help the hive reproduce. She also produces Tentacle Slime, which is a key crafting material.  This Host type provides the foundational materials for growing your colony - protect her at all costs!

### Amor Baroness
Research Tier: Intermediate

A female host specialized to carry Amor Squirmers for use by other host pawns.

### Amor Nymph
Research Tier: Advanced

A female host specialized for seducing and placating other pawns.  Can infect hostile pawns with various debuffs and temporarily stun/down them for ease of capture.  Can also snap friendly pawns out of a mental break.

# Resources

## Animal-Harvestable Resources

### Hard Resin
Hard tentacle resin.  Immune to fire and its jagged edges make excellent cutting tools.

### Soft Resin
Soft tentacle resin.  Pretty and immune to fire, but structurally weak.

### Soft Flesh
A leather substitute that can be used for crafting.  Incredibly soft and feels like genital skin.  (because it is...)

## Animal-Produced Drugs

### Amornuum
A fermented drink that relaxes the pawn, but makes them horny and more likely to engage in sexual activity.  They may also be taken advantage of while in this state, depending on the whims of your pawns...

## Host-Produced Resources

### Tentacle Slime
A sticky, viscous substance produced by Amor Queens.  Used for crafting more advanced eggs and implants.

# Buildings

## Combat and Defense

### Tentacle Turret
Research Tier: Defensive

A portable turret that grows just about anywhere.  Fires low-damage spikes at long range.

### Paralysis Trap
Research Tier: Defensive

A buildable trap that releases a swarm of hostile tentacles when triggered.  Tentacles will attack any nearby pawns (including your own!), so build with care.  They are extremely fragile, but have a powerful paralytic poison.

## Utility

### Amor Calor
Research Tier: Utility

A tentacle structure that generates heat. Requires no fuel or power, but generates less heat than industrial counterparts. The smell is some what suspicious.

### Amor Frigis
Research Tier: Utility

A tentacle structure that generates cold. Requires no fuel or power, but generates less cold than industrial counterparts. The smell is some what suspicious.

### Recycler Pod
Research Tier: Basic

A tentacle structure that is used to recycle "volunteers" into various resources. The process is utterly lethal; the pawn will be torn apart into various resources.

NOTE: Basically a Ripscanner, under the hood.

### Indoctrination Pod
Research Tier: Basic

A tentacle structure that is used to convert captives into obedient slaves. The process will break the pawn's will completely and given sufficient time, may even remove an Unwavering prisoner's resistance.

NOTE: Will be similar to (if not outright a copy of) an Onahole. Will thoroughly violate the pawn and apply the Broken hediff.

### Transformation Pod
Research Tier: Utility, Advanced

A tentacle structure that is used to convert pawns into Boss-tier tentacles. The process is lethal for the pawn; their body will be consumed and used to gestate one of the Boss-tier tentacles.

NOTE: Again, basically a Ripscanner. May need several types, depending on whether or not we can add multiple "loading" abilities.

# Equipment

## Weapons

### Tentacle Whip
A whip crafted from tentacle resin and soft flesh.  Not particularly strong for raw damage output, but will inflict paralytic poison that builds up after repeated strikes.  Definitely Less-Than-Lethal, but can still kill.

### Resin Sword
A sword forged from hard tentacle resin.  Due to the sharp nature of the material, it has high armor penetration.

## Armor
Note that most tentacle armor can only be worn by pawns that have been converted into tentacle hosts, as it interfaces neurally with the tentacles they carry within them for maximum effectiveness.

### Tentacle Slave Collar
A large tentacle, curled tightly around the wearer's neck, intended to use in controlling slaves.  The tip goes into the wearer's mouth, hindering their ability to eat.  The tentacle secretes drugs that provide a mild euphoric effert, suppressing the slave's resistance, increasing their sex drive, and making them more docile.  Not nearly as invasive as an Indoctrination Implant.

### Tentacle Bodysuit
A bodysuit crafted from soft flesh and plasteel.  Provides good protection against all types of damage as well as fire and vaccuum resistance.

### Resin Cuirass
A chestpiece forged from hard tentacle resin.  Provides excellent protection against blunt and sharp damage, but is heavy and will slow down the wearer.

### Resin Helmet
A helmet forged from hard tentacle resin.  Provides excellent protection against blunt and sharp damage.

### Powered Tentacle Armor
A full-body suit of powered armor with a full neural interface connecting to the tentacles within the host.  Provides superior protection against all damage types.  Sealed against vaccuum and toxic environments and heavily resistant to fire.  Has auxillary storage for Amor Squirmers that can be transferred to the host should they run low.

### Powered Tentacle Helmet
A full-head helmet with a neural interface connecting to the tentacles within the host.  Provides superior protection against all damage types.  Sealed against vaccuum and toxic environments and heavily resistant to fire.  Has auxillary storage for Amor Squirmers that can be transferred to the host should they run low.

# Implants

## Amor Squirmer Host Implants

### Uterine Growth Implant (Larva)
Increases the incubation rate of Amor Larva eggs.  Only works on Amor Brood Mothers.

### Uterine Growh Implant (Squirmer)
Increases the incubation rate of Amor Squirmer eggs.  Only works on Amor Spawners.

## Amor Tentacle Incubation Implants

## Amor Host Incubation Implants

### Anal Speculum Implant
A plasteel implant that stretches the host's anus, allowing more capacity for Amor Squirmer storage.

### Stomach Storage Implant
An advanced implant that replaces the host's stomach completely. The Amor Squirmer tentacles that live there will handle the host's digestive processes, which frees up room for additional Amor Squirmer storage capacity.

### Uterine Growth Implant (Queen)
Increases the incubation rate of Amor Brood Mother eggs.  Only works on Amor Queens.

## Misc Implants

### Tentacle Esophagus Replacement
An advanced implant that replaces the host's esophagus with a tentacle-based tube, which will pre-process swallowed food.  This makes the pawn immune to Gut Worms and Food Poisoning.

### Indoctrination Implant
An implant that grafts a small tentacle onto the brainstem.  The tentacle will suppress the pawn's free will to the point where they do not suffer from mental breaks (but cannot receive inspirations).  They need less food and rest, feel less pain, and will basically always be obedient.  This comes with a cost, though, as they suffer a significant penalty to consciousness - and should the tentacle ever be removed, they will die.

### Rut Implant
An implant that grafts a small tentacle onto the brainstem.  The tentacle will kick the user's sexual urges into overdrive as well as providing a significant boost to their work and movement speeds.  While it does make for a hard-working pawn, they will often stop working to engage in lovin'.

# Animal Abilities

## Genital Latch
Usable by Amor Strikers.

Attacks a pawn by leaping onto them and latching onto their genitals.  This will immobilize the pawn by causing massive amounts of pain.  The attacker may choose to let go after a time - or implant things in their victim.

## Larval Transformation
Usable by Amor Larvae.  Allows the player to choose what type of tentacle the Amor Larva will become.  It will enter a metamorphosis cacooon for a time, and then emerge as the chosen type.

# Host Abilities

## Slime Spray
Usable by most Host pawn types.

Ejects a conical spray of Tentacle Slime from the host's mouth, which covers a large area.  It does some damage to what it strikes, but also puts out fires.  It may also trigger uncontrolled sexual urges.

## Seduction Kiss
Usable by Amor Nymphs.

Approaches the target pawn and attempts to seduce them.  If successful, the target will be temporarily non-hostile (if it was hostile) and its Resistance and Willpower values will be significantly reduced.  Can be used to cancel mental breaks, make prisoners more recruitable, or more nefarious deeds.

## Create Amor Womb
Usable by Amor Nymphs.  Only works on pawns that are affected by a Seduction Kiss.

Injects the target pawn with a resinous toxin, which will convert them into an immobile Amor Womb.  The Amor Womb will then be able to store Amor Squirmers for use by other pawns.
